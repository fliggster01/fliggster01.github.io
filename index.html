Our Premium Technology

We use industry-leading chemistry to ensure your vehicle isn't just clean, but protected.

Rain-X® Graphene

The next level of protection. Graphene nanotechnology creates a tighter web of protection than traditional ceramic coatings, offering incredible water beading, slickness, and durability against Minnesota weather.

Armor All® Ceramic Seal

Locks in the shine. This hydrophobic layer bonds to your vehicle's surface, repelling water, dirt, and UV rays to keep your car cleaner for longer and making future washes easier.

Extreme Shine Wax

Real Carnauba wax blend. Delivers a deep, showroom-quality gloss that enhances color depth and provides a sacrificial barrier against road salt, grime, and the elements.

Common Questions

Are you a touchless car wash?

No, we use an advanced soft-cloth system. Touchless car washes often rely on harsh, high-pressure chemicals. Our soft-cloth technology gently and safely removes dirt for a much deeper, superior clean without damaging your clear coat.

How long does the wash take?

We are a true 5-minute express car wash! From the time our team hand-preps your vehicle to the moment you exit the blowers, you'll be back on the road in just minutes.

Is this a full-service car wash?

We offer a full-service clean with the speed of an express wash! Every vehicle receives manual hand-prep from our staff before entering the tunnel, and every wash includes unlimited access to our free high-suction vacuums for your interior.

Is there a contract for the membership?

No long-term contracts! Our memberships are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime through the EverWash app.

Does the wash fit large trucks?

Most standard trucks fit comfortably (max height 7'2"). Dual-wheeled trucks unfortunately cannot fit on the track.

Do you accept cash?

Yes! We accept cash, credit cards, and Apple/Google Pay.
